Prior art we lean on

Japan already validated the method. We didn't have to.

Japan's construction ministry (MLIT) has accepted camera-based rebar measurement since July 2023: bar count, diameter and spacing, with a 5 mm guide on spacing. Vendors such as IHI report ±1 mm diameter and ±5 mm spacing from photos; AIJO reports 70% time saved with a plain tablet camera.

Sariya · validation for free3 / 7
What we built · running on the iQOO 15

AI finds the bars. Maths measures them. Rules decide.

Engineer sends the drawing

Bar size and spacing, signed with a PIN. The app never invents a value.

Operator scans

Live line on every bar, mm between them, scaled by the printed card. Lock fuses ~1.2 s of sharp frames.

Re-scan, never guess

A value within its own error of the limit says "re-scan". Blur and half-seen bars do too.

What a camera can't see

Cover by tape, diameter by weighing an offcut, hooks by template.

Fix for the mason

Spoken in Hindi or Kannada, with subtitles, offline.

Signed record

Ed25519 signed. Engineer reviews and approves with a PIN. Sign-off QR checks offline. Tampering and replays are rejected.

Geometry on a known mesh

50.1 / 49.9 / 64.9 mm measured against 50 / 50 / 65 mm truth. The app never says "safe" or "permit"; it shows numbers, bands and evidence.
