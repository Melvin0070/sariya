## 3. iQOO 15 launch messaging and which hardware claims Sariya can honestly show on a dusty site

### Takeaway
The official iQOO 15 India page markets:
- the 8 Elite Gen 5 with the Q3 chip;
- a 7,000 mAh silicon-anode battery;
- a 6.85-inch 2K 144 Hz OLED with **6,000 nits local, 2,600 global and 1,000 manual full-screen** brightness;
- **IP68 + IP69**;
- the "8K VC" cooling system (iQOO's largest vapour chamber);
- a 50 MP IMX921 main camera with OIS.

Four of these map directly onto a construction site: brightness, dust and water rating, battery, and sustained cooling. Each needs an honest caveat.

### Cited Findings (all V, official page [iqoo.com/in iQOO 15](https://www.iqoo.com/in/products/iqoo15), unless noted)
- **Chip:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (3 nm) plus the "Supercomputing Chip Q3" for graphics, ray tracing, super-resolution and frame interpolation up to 144 FPS. AnTuTu 4,186,917 (iQOO Labs, 16+512 GB).
- **Battery:**
  - 7,000 mAh typical (6,830 rated) silicon-anode battery; 100 W wired; 40 W wireless; bypass charging.
  - Claimed runtimes: 10.5 h of nonstop gaming, 22.6 h of social media.
  - **Battery operating range: -20 °C to 40 °C.**
- **Display:** 6.85-inch 3168×1440, 508 PPI, up to 144 Hz. Peak brightness is 6,000 nits local, 2,600 nits global and 1,000 nits manual full-screen.
- **Durability:** "IP68 + IP69". The footnote says it is not a professional water-resistant phone and protection may decline with wear.
- **Cooling:** 8K VC system, the largest vapour chamber in iQOO's history, with a 14% larger VC area and graphite sheets over 14,000 mm².
- **Cameras:**
  - Main: 50 MP Sony IMX921 (1/1.56-inch) with CIPA 4.5 stabilisation.
  - Periscope: 50 MP IMX882 at 3x.
  - Ultrawide: 50 MP.
- **OriginOS AI features listed:** AI Creation (DocMaster/Notes), AI Captions (live speech-to-text, translate, summarise), AI Season Change.
- **S:** The iQOO 15 starts at ₹85,999 in India. [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/mobile-phones/iqoo-teases-new-flagship-launch-in-india-is-it-iqoo-16.html)

### Inferences: what Sariya can credibly show (and the caveat to say aloud)

| iQOO claim | Site relevance | Honest demo lever | Caveat to state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2,600 nits global / 6,000 nits local | Pre-pour checks happen outdoors, often in the evening sun | Show the overlay and error band readable in direct sun; a high-contrast "re-scan" UI | 1,000 nits is the manual full-screen figure; peak is HDR/local. Don't quote 6,000 as "what you see". |
| IP68 + IP69 | Cement dust, slurry, hosing down | The phone survives a dusty deck; a wipe-clean workflow | iQOO says it is not a professional water-resistant phone. Never splash it on stage (it is a loaner). |
| 7,000 mAh | One engineer, many zones, many sites a day | Measured drain per zone scan from HackTracker/battery stats, extrapolated to "N zones per charge" | Extrapolated, not field-measured over a full day. Measure before claiming. |
| 8K VC cooling + 8 Elite Gen 5 NPU | Back-to-back scans with camera, NPU, TTS/ASR at once | Sustained-run chart: NPU latency (v3 model 12.1 ms per STATE.md) and skin temperature across 30+ consecutive scans, with no throttling cliff | Show your own measured curve; don't borrow iQOO marketing numbers. |
| Battery range -20 to 40 °C | Indian summer decks exceed 40 °C | Admit it: "Above 40 °C on the deck we scan in the evening, which is when pre-pour checks happen anyway" | This is a real limitation; volunteer it before a juror asks. |
| 50 MP IMX921 + OIS | Resolving bar edges and the card at distance | Higher pixels on the card mean a tighter error band | Measured accuracy comes from the error table, not sensor size. |
| Q3 chip | None (graphics/gaming only) | Don't claim it | Claiming it would be fake hardware use. |
| AI Captions (OriginOS) | Spoken fix to the mason | Only if actually used; otherwise say Sariya does its own on-device speech | Don't imply OriginOS integration that isn't there. |

### Gaps
- No independent sustained-NPU thermal test of the iQOO 15 was read; rely on the team's own measurements.
- Whether the IP69 claim covers cement-dust abrasion is unknown; IP ratings test dust ingress, not abrasion.

## 5. Pitch framing for a smartphone-brand CEO, and what to avoid

### Takeaway
Lead with one person and one physical moment (an engineer, a slab, a wrong spacing found the evening before a pour). Then make the phone the hero: "the iQOO 15 becomes a measuring instrument that works all evening in dust and sun, offline". Close on why only a performance phone can do this.

Avoid safety or certification claims, clause-number jargon and unsourced market-size figures. No reliable headcount of Indian site engineers or supervisors was found.

### Cited Findings
- **V:** The brand's stated hackathon purpose is "demanding professional workflows", plus cooling and battery endurance on long tasks. [mediainfoline](https://www.mediainfoline.com/brand/iqoo-announces-indias-phone-first-hackathon-series-challenging-young-innovators-to-build-ai-powered-solutions); [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/489783/iqoo-hackathon-2026-hybrid-mobile-development-challenge.html/amp)
- **S:** The CEO says "start with the consumer"; is wary of trend-chasing tech; and says the majority of buyers don't game and the targets include professionals. [Exhibit](https://www.exhibit.tech/interviews/leaders/nipun-marya-ceo-iqoo-interview-exhibit/); [Smartprix](https://www.smartprix.com/bytes/iqoos-ceo-told-us-about-iqoo-15-chip-originos-6-software-and-ai-plans/)
- **S/U, market size:**
  - Construction-workforce estimates range from about 30 million (CIDC, via ENR, dated) to 70 million+ (job portals, unsourced). [ENR](https://www.enr.com/articles/29783-as-demand-grows-so-does-the-need-for-skilled-workers)
  - Knight Frank's figure of about 8.97 lakh civil-engineer supply (2012-21) is reported secondhand only. [Business Outreach](https://www.businessoutreach.in/?p=72392)
  - **No defensible count of site engineers or supervisors was found.**

### Inferences: concrete, honest pitch levers (for the top-10 pitch)
1. **Hook in his language.** One line at the start: "Most iQOO buyers don't game, and you've said so. Here is a professional for whom this phone's performance is the job." This uses his own public claim as a hook, not as flattery.
2. **Hero moment.** Show a real sabotaged cage, then a tape check on stage that matches the phone, then a re-scan abstention. He is a brand storyteller, so give one memorable frame (the phone over a steel mesh with the error band visible in bright light).
3. **Make the hardware visible with *measured* numbers.** Show four on-screen tiles from the team's own runs:
   - NPU latency (v3 at 12.1 ms);
   - the sustained-scan temperature curve;
   - battery % per zone, giving "zones per charge";
   - "0 bytes sent" while offline.
   Name the features: the 8K VC cooling held the NPU steady, the 7,000 mAh battery covers N zones, and the display stays readable outdoors.
4. **Show "phone as a professional desk".** The Office Kit sign-off (engineer's laptop, two keys, replay attack rejected) mirrors the hackathon's phone + laptop thesis. Say it in one sentence.
5. **The "new reason to buy a flagship" story,** stated as hypothesis and not fact: performance phones as tools for India's construction professionals. Pick one sourced anchor from EVIDENCE.md (e.g. IHB share) rather than a headcount. If asked about market size, say "we don't have a reliable count of site engineers; our pilot path is steel-brand IHB programmes".
6. **Ask him a question back.** "Would iQOO bundle a field kit (printed card + case) through a steel brand's home-builder programme?" This gives a brand CEO something to react to. Label it as an idea.
7. **Mention Qualcomm only briefly.** The model runs on the Hexagon NPU via the Qualcomm stack, which matches the partnership he calls "deep". One phrase, no deep dive.

### What to avoid
- "Safe", "certified", "approved", "permit", "replaces the engineer" (per IDEA.md). Say "measures, flags, abstains; the engineer signs".
- IS 456 / IS 13920 clause numbers, homography or uncertainty-propagation jargon in the CEO pitch. Keep these for the technical jurors' Q&A.
- Quoting iQOO's 6,000-nit or IP69 marketing as Sariya's own proof, or splashing the loaner phone.
- Claiming the Q3 chip or OriginOS AI features are used if they aren't.
- "Make in India" via iQOO (unverified), and unsourced headcounts ("10 million site engineers").
- Mentioning iQOO's falling market share or the iQOO 16 cancellation rumours.
- Any claim that the CEO is a juror, in public material (unverified).

### Gaps
- No authoritative count of civil engineers, site supervisors or contractors in India. PLFS/NCO occupation tables or NSDC construction-sector skill reports would be the primary sources and were not checked.
- No evidence of what Marya personally rewarded at past judging (only that he chose a Chief Gaming Officer); his judging preferences are inferred from interviews.
