**One-line answer to "who pays":** the organisations that already send a paid person to the pour. Steel brands first (a FAIL for missing bars sells steel and the scan verifies their brand on site), cement brands' technical services second, organised builders and inspectors third at a per-site price. Lenders buy the evidence layer only. PMAY is an impact footnote.

## 2. Unit economics sketch

**Cost per site.**
- On-device inference, zero marginal [U by design].
- Fiducial card and strip: A4 laminated, ₹15-40 printed in bulk; one card per site, re-usable per member [U].
- Engineer time: 8-12 min per member for a scan, tape prompts and voice confirmations [H, measure at the Bengaluru visits].
- Backend for signed records: a few KB per record; ₹1-2 per site/year at cloud prices [U].
- Support and rulebook maintenance: one engineer-reviewer per 2,000 sites/yr [H].

**Three-year revenue sketch, brand channel only** (assumptions stated; all [H]):
- Year 1: one regional brand pilot (free, 50 pours) plus one national brand paid pilot: 100 engineer seats × ₹2,000 × 6 months = **₹12 lakh**.
- Year 2: one national steel brand at 500 seats × ₹2,000 × 12 = ₹1.2 Cr; one cement technical-services team at 300 seats × ₹1,500 × 12 = ₹54 lakh; two regional brands at ₹15 lakh each = ₹30 lakh. **≈ ₹2.0 Cr**.
- Year 3: two national steel brands (1,000 seats, ₹2.4 Cr), two cement majors (800 seats, ₹1.4 Cr), regional brands ₹60 lakh, per-record white-label reports 1 lakh records × ₹50 = ₹50 lakh. **≈ ₹4.9 Cr**.
- Sensitivity: the seat price is the whole model. At ₹1,000/seat Year 3 halves; at ₹3,000 it is ₹7 Cr. Seats are capped by brand field forces (UltraTech 1,200 civil engineers [V]; Tata "100+" customer-service engineers [S, earlier]); the ceiling across all majors is perhaps 5,000-8,000 seats [U].
- Builders, inspectors and lenders are additive but smaller: 2,000 Bengaluru sites/yr × ₹750 = ₹15 lakh in Year 2 [H].

## 5. Risks

**Liability.** The signed record states what was measured, with what error, by whom, when and where, and which items were "within limits", "outside limits", "needs a tape reading" or "not seen". It does not claim the member, floor or building is safe, that the drawing is right, or that anything unmeasured (formwork, props, laps hidden behind other steel, bottom cover without a tape entry) is acceptable. The engineer's sign-off is the engineer's professional act under their own key; Sariya is the instrument. Terms: instrument-grade claims only, an error table published in the app, no "pass" word anywhere (see notes/11-name/name-check.md §2). Keep professional-indemnity insurance in the plan for Year 2 [U on cost].

**Engineer resistance.** A record is also evidence against the engineer who signs it. Mitigations: the engineer chooses what to sign; "needs a tape reading" is an honest state, not a failure; the record protects the engineer who flagged and fixed. The Taratala arrests [S] cut both ways: some engineers will want the record, some will refuse to create any. Pilot C tests which.

**Brand conflict.** A FAIL for missing bars sells steel; a FAIL that says "bars underweight" or "rib marks do not match the invoice" accuses the brand's own channel (or a counterfeiter). Rule: the weigh test and rib-mark check report numbers and "does not match", never "fake"; the brand's own helpline handles the next step. Contract clause: the brand cannot suppress a measurement, only its branding on the report.

**Data ownership.** The owner owns the record of their house; the operator's organisation gets a licence to the records their staff created; Sariya gets de-identified geometry and error data for the model and the defect statistic. Faces and house location stay on device unless the owner shares the report. No brand sees another brand's sites. Put this in the pilot MoU before day one.

**Other.** A brand clones it (likely within 18 months of a public win): the defence is the rulebook, the error data and being first with the field teams. A false alarm on stage or in a pilot costs more than ten correct calls: keep the live claim to count, spacing and stirrup zones (VERDICT.md).
