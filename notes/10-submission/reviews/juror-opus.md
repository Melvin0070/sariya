(1) Submit **Sariya**: it has the best rubric case of the six. A judge places the fault, tapes the number the phone drew, and watches it say "re-scan" when unsure. The NPU does load-bearing segmentation rather than decoration, and the demo survives a noisy hall because nothing depends on audio. The 48-hour scope is bounded by the card and its homography (the geometry that sets real-world scale), and no rival in the field is likely to collide with it.

(2) Runner-up: **Drishti**. It has the strongest dated "why now" and the best inclusion story, but only if the team proves TTS-engine capture alongside TalkBack on an iQOO/vivo phone before submitting, and cuts the replay agent to a stretch goal. If that capture is not proven, it drops below Tape Check.
