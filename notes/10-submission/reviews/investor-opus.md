(1) **Submit Sariya:** it is the only idea with an empty Indian space, a credible payer whose budget already exists (brand engineers already visit slab castings), a physical demo a judge can check with a tape measure, and an NPU that is actually needed rather than decorative. It must lead with real pre-pour site data, not claims about collapses.

(2) **Runner-up: Drishti.** It has the strongest dated legal trigger and a real compliance budget behind it, but it is a workflow tool inside a services market that already exists, and it is too risky to build in 48 hours on an untested OS.
