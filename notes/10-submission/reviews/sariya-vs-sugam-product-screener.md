**(1) Submit Sariya:** it is the one idea I have not seen today, no city winner touches construction, the problem line sticks, and the camera plus NPU model is the product rather than an add-on, whereas Sugam's best sentence is undercut by a thin AI core and a category (accessibility maps, citizen evidence reports) every screener has seen.

**(2) Three changes to Sariya's title and description**

1. **Title and first line a non-engineer gets.** "Sariya: check the steel before the concrete hides it." Follow with one pictured user and moment: a site engineer, the evening before the slab pour, one phone and a printed card, no signal.
2. **Cut the scope in the text to what 48 hours can show.** Name two measured checks only (bar count and spacing on a slab; stirrup spacing by zone on a beam), each with an error band and "re-scan" when too close. List cover, diameter and hooks as prompted manual readings. Drop payers, brands, lenders, two-key detail and roadmap. This answers the "can they build it" doubt, which is the main risk.
3. **State the phone-first and model facts plainly, and only claims you can stand behind.** One line: open segmentation model on the Snapdragon NPU, on-device Hindi/Kannada voice, Office Kit review and sign-off on the laptop, works in airplane mode. One line of precedent ("camera rebar inspection is already used in Japan") for feasibility. Use your own site count if you have it; otherwise remove "most houses" and keep collapse statistics out. Say "measures and records", never "certifies".
