# Extract and track live bar lines in card coordinates

Moving a real bar moves its on-screen line and approximate mm gap without producing a premature verdict.

## Ownership and deliverable

Own **segment/ frame adapter, geometry/ live extraction**. You are not alone in the codebase: preserve others' changes. Alwin owns UI, Sabari camera/geometry, Melvin shared types/build/rules/trust. Request a handoff before editing another package or frozen contract.

## Work

- Warp probabilities/mask to the card plane, remove card edges, derive per-orientation profiles and peaks, fit visible centrelines and associate stable family/slot IDs in mm. Use Hough/skeleton fallback only if needed.
- Preserve input-to-model-to-card-to-preview transforms; publish immutable LiveFrameResult with timestamp, approximate gaps, coverage, gates, backend and per-stage latency.
- Reject tie-wire/shadow/card false positives and retain visibility/occlusion status rather than filling missing bars from the expected count. Do not make the model match the drawing.
- Only do bounded work per newest frame; measure throughput. Target >=10 live updates/s on the chosen backend and use real thermal controls, not synthetic load.
- Handle the F2 80 mm gap together with its adjacent 20 mm gap: the two close bars must not merge and total count must remain 5.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Fixture masks for control, missing bar, crossing families, card edge and F2 give expected topology without injecting expected bars.
- [ ] Motion, out-of-order frames and lost-card results do not retain a valid live measurement flag; no geometry from the future/previous spec leaks into Lock.
- [ ] Live messages contain approximate values only; no rule verdict is computed on this path.

**Loaner check:** Move T4 from 190 to 220 and back while the phone stays still, then move the phone. Lines follow the bars and the overlay/card remain aligned.
