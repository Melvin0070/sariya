# Reject duplicate approvals without blocking legitimate rescans

Yesterday’s already-approved pack cannot be counted or approved again, while a genuine new observation remains possible.

## Ownership and deliverable

Own **record/ duplicate, revision and freshness policies**. You are not alone in the codebase: preserve others' changes. Alwin owns UI, Sabari camera/geometry, Melvin shared types/build/rules/trust. Request a handoff before editing another package or frozen contract.

## Work

- Persist processed record IDs, canonical hashes and approval associations across restarts. Reimport may open history idempotently; a second approval/counting action must say already processed.
- Reject reuse of an old approval on a different hash or spec revision. Freshness/member identity belongs in the signed payload; wall-clock age alone is not proof of a replay.
- Treat perceptual similarity as a review warning, not an automatic hard block. A needed re-scan of unchanged steel naturally looks like the previous image.
- Separate integrity/duplicate checking from physical-scene authenticity. The demo has one card S; do not claim card 20 proves a unique site or that this prevents photographing a screen.
- Keep the exact rejected/flagged reason visible in the review UI through its state contract.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Already-approved pack rejected for another approval after restart. Import of the same pack remains safe/idempotent and does not inflate scan counters.
- [ ] New record/new capture of the unchanged mesh is allowed with a similarity warning if enabled. Corrected F2 is not blocked by perceptual similarity alone.
- [ ] Old approval attached to modified spec or photo fails; unknown enrolled identity remains distinct from duplicate record.

**Loaner check:** Approve one record, reopen it and attempt approval again; then create a legitimate new Lock of the same prop and review it successfully.

## Cut line / failure branch

Ship deterministic record/hash duplicate protection first. Cut perceptual hash implementation before weakening legitimate re-scan behavior or inventing liveness.
