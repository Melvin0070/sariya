## Work

- Detect IDs 450..461, 20 mm markers, 25 mm pitch; centres are 12.5+(id-450)*25 mm from the marked zero end. Establish strip orientation and left-end origin, including reversed-view detection.
- Read the demo drawing: 300 mm beam face; pieces at 25/75/125/200/275; left end first 150 at 50, mid at 75. Do not invent a symmetric right-end zone in this small demo or overlap zones silently.
- Calculate spacing, count and first-link offset per actually covered zone; mask the strip and reject occlusion. Use min line length 80 mm for the 125 mm cross pieces.
- Return typed zones and FixAction via B05, not UI heuristics. F3 moves piece 75 to about 180 (not 190, where masks can merge).
- Expose drawing-vs-code origin: this prop is a drawing-comparison demonstration, not a scale model proving structural code compliance.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Strip centre fixtures for first/last IDs and flipped orientation pass. Mixed/full-scale marker IDs cannot silently use half-scale scale.
- [ ] CONTROL positions and F3 [25,125,180,200,275] yield end gap 100, two end links and add 1; incomplete zone is not seen.
- [ ] Band terms and lock evidence are preserved; a hidden/merged piece is not silently counted from the drawing.
