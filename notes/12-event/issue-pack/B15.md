## Work

- Implement push-to-talk ASR only if usable offline assets exist; ask Sabari for artifact/runtime help without transferring ownership of his vision lane. Typed entry remains immediately available.
- Parse constrained Hindi/English numbers and field names into candidates; preserve raw transcript and unknown fields. Accept end zone 100..1500 step 25 and spacing 25..400 step 5, including demo end zone 150; show out-of-set candidates for correction rather than silently rounding.
- Read back every changed field and require explicit confirmation before creating a new SpecRevision. Voice cannot overwrite a signed record or generate a verdict.
- Serialize ASR and TTS around camera inference when needed; release microphone after push-to-talk. Handle hall noise, missing language and cancellation.
- Keep Gemma/Kannada generation in B23; deterministic parsing/templates satisfy this issue.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Tests cover 50 vs 150, 8 vs 80, mixed-language numerals, missing keyword, negative and out-of-range values; uncertain values remain unconfirmed.

- [ ] Network-disabled recognition works or a visible keypad fallback is chosen within the timebox.
