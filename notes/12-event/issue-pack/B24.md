## Work

- Recruit a practising engineer to review rule applicability, project tolerances, disclosure language and the manual-reading protocol. Record review status; do not imply endorsement before it occurs.
- Run consented tape-ground-truth field trials across lighting, bar sizes, distances, planes and operators; separate calibration/training/holdout sets. Track false confident verdicts and abstentions, not only mean error.
- Have Sabari assess domain shift/retraining and Alwin observe task completion, correction comprehension and repeated record use. Define acceptance criteria with the engineer before judging the field trial.
- Document enrolled-device identity vs professional identity, revocation/replacement, duplicate policy, retention/deletion, image privacy/redaction and safe export before real customer data.
- Test the paid-user hypothesis with brand technical engineers/builders; turn actual pilot findings into scoped follow-up issues. No invented partners or site-prevalence statistic.
