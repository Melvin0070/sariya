## Work

- At 01:00 Sun freeze features; later changes are blocker fixes, measured numbers and assets. Freeze a candidate before dawn Red and keep last-known-good APK available.
- Run meaningful rule/canonical/security/transform tests plus release-mode build, lifecycle smoke and complete device flow. Check shrinker/native libraries/model packaging, not just debug APK.
- Preserve signing continuity: an incompatible release APK cannot be installed over debug without losing Android Keystore keys on uninstall. Use the tested final package/certificate plan; if a reinstall is necessary, export evidence and deliberately re-enrol keys before rehearsal.
- Write README build/demo steps and implemented/deferred features, PROVENANCE with actual app/training timestamps, MODEL_CARD with shipped checksum/licence/data/runtime measurements, and LICENSES. Research repo history before kickoff is not app provenance; disclose prepared assets accurately.
- Attach APK and checksum/commit, model acquisition or bundled-asset instructions, deck PDF, real video and bench CSV. Keep private signing keys/secrets and unconsented personal images out of the public submission.
- Prepare all submission fields and confirm the floor cutoff. Hand the exact candidate/checksums to B26 for final rehearsal; Melvin submits after its verdict by the earlier of 11:30 Sun or 30 minutes before the announced cutoff. This issue closes on candidate delivery, not submission receipt.
- Before returning loaners, export team evidence and use approved steps to sign out personal accounts/remove team secrets; do not alter HackTracker.

- [ ] Cold start with network disabled -> typed spec -> scan -> lock -> fix -> saved evidence succeeds on all three loaners in the selected mode.
