## Work

- Bind deterministic findings and FixAction to the verdict sheet; highlight the specific layer/gap in the frozen evidence. Show within/outside/re-scan/needs tape/not seen without a global safety badge.
- Use deterministic Hindi templates and subtitles. First use a verified offline installed voice or correct prerecorded/template audio; Piper can be wired if already available. Do not block the loop on a generative LLM.
- Keep speech numbers and units identical to the finding; dynamic number words must cover the actual values. A prerecorded fixed phrase must never announce an unmeasured value.
- Add editable manual cover, offcut length/mass and hook observations with explicit source. Missing tools or hidden surfaces remain needs-tape/not-seen. Physical prompts do not imply camera measurements.
- Add stop/repeat speech; microphone/TTS do not run continuously for scoring. System voice missing -> subtitles plus visible audio-unavailable state.
