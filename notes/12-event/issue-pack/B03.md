## Ownership and deliverable

Own **ui/spec/, ui/scan/ shell and navigation**. You are not alone in the codebase: preserve others' changes. Alwin owns UI, Sabari camera/geometry, Melvin shared types/build/rules/trust. Request a handoff before editing another package or frozen contract.

## Work

- Own all Compose screen layout; service owners expose state/actions rather than editing ui/. Build against B01 fake results, then replace the provider at integration.
- Make typed entry the reliable baseline: slab diameter/count/spacing per family; beam stirrup diameter/end spacing/mid spacing/end-zone length. The old five-field shorthand must not lose member type, units, count or zone information.
- Ship the half-scale presets as editable demo data: slab 8 mm, 5 bars per family at 50; beam 8 mm rings, end 50 over 150, mid 75. Display DEMO PROP where appropriate.
- Provide explicit measurement-only mode with no drawing; missing fields never silently inherit a passing default. Confirm and version the spec before Lock.
- Design readable mm values, non-colour-only statuses, large touch targets, subtitles and space clear of the HackTracker overlay. Camera/microphone permission recovery and offline asset state must be reachable.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Forms reject nonfinite/negative/impossible inputs, preserve mm units and accept end zone 150. Cancel/edit does not commit an unconfirmed spec.
- [ ] No-drawing mode cannot show within/outside or infer a drawing requirement.
- [ ] UI previews cover loading, live, locking, locked, re-scan, not-seen, needs-tape, permission denied and manual mode.

**Loaner check:** On a loaner, enter both presets and a custom spacing; rotate/resume; confirm data survives navigation and text is readable in the laptop mirror.
