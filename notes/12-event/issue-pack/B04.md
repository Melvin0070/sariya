# Capture calibrated frames and detect card S with stream-aware gates

Every measurement is associated with a valid card pose and the exact camera geometry that produced it.

**Owner:** @NarayanaSabari · **Phase:** CP1 · **Budget:** 2 focused person-hours (estimate; excludes idle training/download time)
**Control and current gate times:** [CONTROL.md](CONTROL.md)
**Prerequisites / integration dependencies:** [B01](B01.md)
Work against B01 fixtures while a service dependency is being built; acceptance still needs the real service. The control issue defines the explicit manual/gated fallback path. Do not close an unproven automatic feature just because the fallback shipped.

## Ownership and deliverable

Own **capture/, fiducial/ card path**. You are not alone in the codebase: preserve others' changes. Alwin owns UI, Sabari camera/geometry, Melvin shared types/build/rules/trust. Request a handoff before editing another package or frozen contract.

## Work

- Use CameraX/Camera2 on the main camera with bounded backpressure and guaranteed frame cleanup. Validate 4:3 framing or sufficient actual coverage for the 220 mm grid zone; do not measure off-screen geometry.
- Detect DICT_5X5_1000 card S IDs 360..377, 6x6 squares, nominal square 15 mm/marker 11 mm. Store measured pattern width/6, not just nominal print size. Mask the card footprint out of bar extraction.
- Calibrate the chosen measurement stream, including crop and rotation. Do not apply 4K intrinsics unchanged to 1080p. Record calibration residual or mark uncalibrated with increased uncertainty.
- Resolve the v4 preview versus old 4K-still quality gate before geometry: 45 px is a 4K reference, not a raw threshold for every stream. A normalized threshold must use actual transforms and be validated with tape; upsampling does not create precision.
- Check card validity, coverage, reprojection, tilt, blur, gyro and marker resolution. If no valid card, expose reason and do not permit a new Lock. An old anchored overlay is not fresh measurement evidence.
- Keep ARCore outside the baseline; B22 is a separate 20-minute optional probe. Keep camera ownership singular.
- Before B06/B07 implementation, publish LOCK-MODE in the control issue: actual stream/resolution, calibration/transforms, evidence frame source, quality metric and fallback. Expose calibrated frozen-frame pixel-to-mm/manual-measurement adapter with conservative band before learned geometry; Alwin owns the marking UI.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Transform fixtures for rotation 0/90/180/270 and crop/letterbox map known points correctly. Invalid/inverted pose, missing card and partial coverage abstain.
- [ ] CONTROL at intended 0.30-0.35 m can be measured only when actual capture quality supports it; the 0.8 m case must abstain. Save effective intrinsics, frame dimensions and gate metrics for both.
- [ ] No permanent frame retention or stalled preview after 100 frames, background/resume or permission failure.

**Loaner check:** Scan near/far and tilted card S, partly cover it, move it out of frame, and resume from Office Kit. Inspect gate reasons and capture geometry logs.

## Cut line / failure branch

If preview resolution cannot support the measured band, keep approximate live lines and use a validated high-resolution capture at Lock; record this downgrade explicitly, with its own image and mode. No silent threshold weakening.
