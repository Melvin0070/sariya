Sariya UX flows

One pre-pour check, across three phones and a laptop. The operator scans the steel, the engineer signs off with their own key, and anyone can check the sign-off offline.

This is the designed spec as of 10 Oct. Every build issue is still open, so none of these screens are confirmed as built.

End to end, by device

The scan screen, state by state

Every check gets one of five answers

✓ within limits ✕ outside limits ↻ re-scan ▭ needs a tape reading – not seen

No overall badge. Never shown or spoken: safe · PASS · certified · permit

The flows, step by step

0 · First launch

B02 · B12 · B13
All phones, once
  1. Readiness screen shows the model compiling, not a frozen camera.
  2. Camera or microphone denied: a visible way to recover. Offline voice and model assets show as present or missing.
  3. Each phone shows its key QR and scans the others, confirmed by fingerprint. The real protection level is shown.
  4. A key that arrives inside a pack stays untrusted until enrolled this way.

1 · Spec in

B03 · B15 · B21
P1 operator
  1. Choose slab or beam.
  2. Type the drawing values. Slab: diameter, count and spacing per direction. Beam: stirrup diameter, end and mid spacing, end-zone length.
  3. Or hold to talk: “rings 8, 50 at the end, 75 middle, end zone 150”. Every field is read back; doubtful values stay unconfirmed.
  4. Confirm to create a versioned spec. Cancel commits nothing.
  5. No drawing: measurement-only mode, values with no verdict.

2 · Scan and Lock

B04 · B06 · B07 · B08 · B11
P1 operator
  1. Card on the slab patch, or strip_300 along the beam. Torch on, about 30 cm away.
  2. Lines snap to each bar with live “~mm” gaps and follow a bar when it moves.
  3. Hold still to Lock: the image freezes with “80 ± 3 mm c/c”.
  4. Beam: the strip origin, ticks and end and mid zones are drawn, with stirrup positions per zone.
  5. If detection is unstable, switch to manual mode and tap the bars.

3 · Verdict and fix

B05 · B09
P1, spoken to the mason
  1. The verdict sheet lists each check, with the failing gap highlighted on the frozen image.
  2. The fix is spoken in Hindi with subtitles: “Beam B2, left end: stirrups at 180, drawing says 100 for the first 600 mm. Add 2.” Stop and repeat buttons.
  3. No voice installed: subtitles stay and “audio unavailable” shows.
  4. Readings by hand: cover by tape, offcut length and mass (200 mm at 123 g is a 10, not a 12), and the hook against a template. Each edit makes a new revision.
  5. The bench tape panel turns a locked gap and its tape reading into an error-table row.

4 · Sign and review

B12 · B13 · B14
P1 → laptop → P2
  1. P1 signs the capture: the values, spec revision, rule and model versions, image hashes and manual readings.
  2. Export .sariya.zip through Office Kit. P2 opens it from the system picker; the hashes and signature are checked first.
  3. The review desk shows the frozen overlay, drawing, values with bands, sources, missing checks, signers and revision.
  4. Approve with a fingerprint (cancel means no signature), or request another view of a named zone.
  5. Approve is disabled for corrupt, unknown-signer or unreviewed packs.

5 · Verify and replay

B14 · B16
P1 · P3
  1. The approval returns through Office Kit and attaches only to the capture it signed.
  2. P3 scans the sign-off QR and checks it offline against enrolled keys.
  3. Approving an approved pack again shows “already processed”.
  4. An old approval on an edited spec or photo fails.
  5. A new scan of unchanged steel is allowed, with at most a similarity warning.

6 · History and numbers

B18
Any phone
  1. Saved scans by member, time and status: reopen or export.
  2. The error table: measured against tape, residual, band, abstentions, distance and source. Exports as CSV.
  3. Timings: NPU, GPU and CPU p50 and p95, the accelerator actually used, the model hash and sample counts.
  4. Every value comes from stored samples. No data shows as “no data”, never as zero error.

Rules for every screen

  • Large touch targets; text readable through the laptop mirror.
  • Status is never shown by colour alone.
  • Keep space clear for the HackTracker overlay.
  • Anything marked by hand, missing or unassessed says so.
  • A signed record is never changed in place; edits make a new revision.
  • The mason hears the fix and never holds the phone.
