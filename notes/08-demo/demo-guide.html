Sariya demo guide

The steel inside an Indian house is visible for a day, then the concrete hides it for good. Sariya checks it with one phone and a printed card, offline: it counts the bars, measures the spacing with an error band, tells the mason the fix in Hindi, and the engineer signs the record.

High level

The loop, step by step

1voice · form

Spec in

Five numbers per member from the drawing: bar size, bar count or spacing, ring size, ring spacing at the end and middle, end-zone length. Each is read back and confirmed.

→ Slab S1: 8 mm @ 50, 5 × 5
2camera

Scan

Card on the slab bars, strip along the beam. Hold the phone over the steel for 2–3 s, torch on. The app grabs sharp frames by itself.

→ sharp 4K frames with the card in view
3NPU

Measure

Finds the steel pixels, turns each bar into a centre line, converts to millimetres using the card. Every gap gets a ± band.

→ gaps 50 · 50 · 80 ± 3 · 20
4tape · scale

Manual checks

What a camera can't see: cover by tape (spoken), bar size by weighing a 20 cm offcut, hooks by template.

→ 123 g over 200 mm = 10 mm class
5plain code

Decide

Fixed rules, no AI. Drawing first, then IS 456 and IS 13920. Each check: within, outside, re-scan, needs a tape reading, or not seen.

→ gap 3 outside limits (80 vs 65)
6voice out

Tell the mason

One short sentence per problem, in Hindi or Kannada, with subtitles. Numbers come straight from step 5.

→ "Teesra gap assi millimetre hai…"
7keys

Sign

The record is signed on the operator's phone, reviewed on the laptop over Office Kit, and signed by the engineer with their own fingerprint.

→ QR the mason's phone verifies offline

Steps 2 and 3 happen together: the measuring runs live while the phone is held over the steel.

The demo run

0:00
The one-day window

"This steel is visible for a day, then concrete covers it. Japan has accepted camera checks since 2023; we built it for India, on one phone. Half-scale props; the app checks whatever the drawing says."

0:30
The judge sabotages

A judge draws a fault card and does it without telling us: pulls a bar, opens a gap to about 80 mm, or moves a beam ring.

0:45
Scan

Phone 1 at about 30 cm, torch on. Live overlay on the bars, coverage ticking.

0:55
Verdict, spoken

"Gap 3: 80 ± 3 mm. Drawing 50. Outside limits." Hindi voice, Hindi and English subtitles.

1:05
The judge tapes it

The phone highlights the gap it measured; the judge tapes it centre to centre and reads about 80.

1:25
Honesty

Gap opened to 72 (limit 65). From 0.8 m: "Re-scan, too far to be sure." From 0.3 m: "72 ± 3, outside."

1:50
Both directions

A lower-layer bar is moved. The app sees it through the gaps: "Lower bars, gap 2 is 80 ± 4, outside."

2:10
Weigh test

The offcut taped "12" weighs 123 g. "It's a 10. A 12 weighs 178."

2:30
The engineer signs

Record lands on the laptop over Office Kit; the engineer reviews the frame and signs on Phone 2 with a fingerprint.

2:48
Verify and replay

Phone 3 scans the QR offline: valid. Yesterday's record is re-sent: rejected.

3:00
Numbers and the ask

NPU vs GPU vs CPU timings, scans, battery, bench accuracy. Who pays: steel and cement brands. The ask: one brand, 20 sites, 30 days.

Trade-off

What half scale costs

Only the real-world look. Every check, voice line, tape check and signing step works the same. Say "half-scale props" first, before a judge asks. If asked "does it work at real scale?": the bars are a real 8 mm thick but the gaps are half size, so this is the harder test. The re-scan beat triggers on the small card being too far rather than on the band straddling the limit, and bench accuracy is quoted at 50 mm spacing.
