01
Who can participate?

Students and working professionals (IT and white collar) across India. Every track is open to both buckets, but teams cannot mix: a team is either all students or all working professionals. Register solo or as a team of 1–3. You attend one city battle in person after shortlisting.
=====
02
What is the format?

A 30-hour phone-first battle: Saturday 10:00 to Sunday around 17:00. Active hacking from 11:00 with Green Light (both devices) and Red Light (iQOO phone only via Office Kit). Two evaluation rounds feed a Top 10 pitch, then awards.
=====
03
How do city battles and the finale connect?

Top six teams from each city advance to the Grand Finale in Bengaluru on Oct 9–11, 2026: three student teams and three working-professional teams. The Finale runs 48 hours, Friday evening to Sunday evening — longer than the 30-hour city weekend. Standout teams beyond the Top 6 can also earn Finale slots, and you can register directly for the Grand Finale without going through a city battle. Four city battles, one national title.
=====
04
Can I enter the Grand Finale without doing a city battle?

Yes. Registrations open directly for the Grand Finale, so winning or even entering a city battle is not a prerequisite. The city route stays the larger one: the top six teams from each city, three student and three working professional, advance automatically, and standout teams beyond the Top 6 can also earn slots. Direct entries are screened the same way city entries are.
=====
05
What can I build?

City battles run seven tracks: FinTech and Commerce, Smart Education, HealthTech, Productivity, Smart Living, Developer Tools and Open Innovation. The Grand Finale runs six: Mobility, Community App, Smart Living, Productivity, Developer Tools and Open Innovation. Tracks are broad domains, not fixed briefs. A local or open-source model at the core earns brownie points; keep the phone in the loop via Office Kit. Open Innovation is the wildcard everywhere.
=====
06
What is the prize pool?

The total pool is ₹40,00,000 across the four city battles and the Grand Finale. Each city targets ₹6L split across working professionals (₹3.2L) and students (₹2.8L). Final numbers are set by iQOO.
=====
07
What stack can I use?

Anything that runs on the phone: native Android, Flutter, React Native, or PWA. You demo on the iQOO device, and a local or open-source model at the core earns brownie points. On-device inference targets the Snapdragon NPU (Sarvam, Gemma, Phi class models). Office Kit bridges to the laptop when you need deeper compute.
=====
08
How does shortlisting work?

Registration is open to anyone, and every entry goes through idea screening to shortlist builders for the on-ground battle. Scoring weights novelty, tech impact, problem choice, idea/scope fit for 30 hours, and phone-first fit. Registration is free.
=====
