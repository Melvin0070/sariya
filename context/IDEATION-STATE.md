## Kill-shots (apply to every idea in Session B)
- Collides with one of the 24 city winners or a crowded repo cluster (DIGEST section 7).
- Duplicates a feature a phone maker already ships (DIGEST section 7).
- Depends on ARCore, ToF, a barometer or UWB on the iQOO 15 without an on-device test.
- Claims a measurement without a scale reference or with an error band tighter than DIGEST section 4 allows.
- Needs a closed or non-commercial model at the core (Sarvam Edge, FastVLM, Depth Pro, sarvam-1).
- Rests on a mandate that is not in force, is NCR-only or state-unadopted, or whose official capture point is already a government app.
- Cannot be built mostly on the phone during 18.5 h of Red Light, or earns no HackTracker signal (camera app opens, Office Kit time, steady input).
- No "oh wow" moment in the live demo (user's rule).
- No named budget owner; answers "who pays?" with a guess.
- Sells "on-device" as the benefit instead of the outcome; no opened source shows a buyer paying for local inference by itself (research/05).
- Buyer is a small merchant or housing society with no stated willingness to pay (Vyapar ₹699 a year; MyGate mostly advertising revenue).
- Is an investor "open request" that research/05 found already built (human-proof for calls, elder companion, freelancer accountant, PNR and crop-price voice, children's tutor).
- Core model needs an NPU build that does not exist for SM8850 (Gemma 4 E2B is GPU-only on this chip).
- Is one of the per-track hackathon cliches listed in research/06a-06c.
- Assumes a Gemini Nano feature works in an Indian language or in the background (it does not on the iQOO 15).
