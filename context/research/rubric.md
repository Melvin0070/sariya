## 2. Judging rubric and weights

Source: Home "How we score"; Guide "How we score" and "Judging". One rubric, stated to be identical across all four cities and both buckets. 75% jury panel, 25% HackTracker device data.

| Criterion (exact) | Scored by | Weight | What it covers (restated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| End product quality | Jury | 30% | Whether it works, is useful, and someone would keep using it. |
| Novelty and impact | Jury | 20% | Originality and real-world impact. |
| HackTracker · creative phone use | Device data | 15% | Camera, voice, on-device AI in the build. |
| Technical depth | Jury | 15% | Architecture, code quality, robustness, real use of the hardware. |
| HackTracker · Office Kit usage | Device data | 10% | Phone and laptop bridge use. |
| Demo and presentation | Jury | 10% | A compelling 3 to 5 minute pitch. |
| **Total** | | **100%** | |

Stated alongside the rubric:

- A well-built simple product is said to beat a broken complex one.
- The 25% device-data share is read from HackTracker, not self-reported. HackTracker records counts and durations only (no keystrokes, screenshots or browsing).
- Two scored evaluation rounds feed a Top 10; the final pitch decides the winners; the demo runs on the iQOO phone. (The Finale schedule lists three scored checkpoints; how three checkpoints map to "two rounds" at the Finale is [U].)
- The jury sees the demo cold at the end. Home page says a five-person jury drawn from product, AI/ML and the Reskilll network. Finale panel: not announced.
- Exactly how HackTracker converts counts and durations into the 15% and 10% scores is not published. **[U]**

## 5. Rules

Source: Guide "Rules & guidelines"; Terms clauses 02–08.

Eligibility
- Students and working professionals (FAQ: IT and white collar) across India. Free to enter.
- Teams of 1–3. A team is all students or all working professionals; no mixing. Buckets are judged separately. Solo entrants may be matched into a team at the organisers' discretion.
- Under-18s may need written parental or guardian consent to attend.
- Organisers may ask for proof of identity, age, student status or employment at any stage, and may reject or disqualify if it is not provided.
- In person only.

Phone-first
- The iQOO device is the build surface and the demo surface. Every entry must run and pitch on the phone.
- If it runs on the phone with a local or open-source model at the core, it qualifies; that core earns brownie points.

Build rules
- Original work only: code written during the event window. Shipping a pre-built product or carrying in a completed app is not allowed.
- Pre-event drafting of ideas is permitted (Terms clause 03).
- Open-source libraries and frameworks are fine with attribution. Third-party APIs are fine subject to their licences and attribution.
- Whether pre-trained or pre-converted model weights, fine-tunes and datasets prepared before the event are allowed is not stated. The site itself expects off-the-shelf Sarvam, Gemma and Phi class models. Custom pre-event fine-tunes: **[U]**
- Submit repo and demo assets on the Reskilll platform before the hard cut-off. Repos are locked before the Top 10 pitches. Late submissions may be penalised or disqualified.
- Organisers may verify that a project was built inside the event window.

Disqualification grounds
- Cheating, plagiarism, unfair practice, or misrepresenting authorship.
- Submitting work developed for, or submitted to, another competition (Terms clause 03).
- Ineligibility; incomplete, false, misleading or duplicated information; breach of the code of conduct or terms; non-compliance with venue, safety or organiser directions (Terms clause 08).
- Organisers may also decline, cancel or disqualify at their sole discretion without giving a reason, and may change tracks, prizes and schedule at any time (Terms clause 08).

Loaner device
- Remains iQOO property, loaned for the event only. Keep it in the venue or hacking zone and return it before exit.
- Do not modify, unlock, encumber or part with it. Loss, theft or damage is recoverable from the team at replacement value. (Implication: no bootloader unlock or root.)

IP and data
- The team keeps IP. Organisers get a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide, irrevocable licence to review, evaluate, publish and showcase the submission, team name and city for event purposes. Commercial exploitation beyond showcase needs a separate written agreement.
- The team warrants the submission does not infringe third-party rights and indemnifies the organisers.

Judging and prizes
- Jury decisions are final. Prize money goes to the registered team leader, less TDS. Organisers need not award a prize if no entry meets the standard.

Conduct and venue
- Respect; zero tolerance for harassment. No smoking or alcohol on site. Stay in designated zones. Do not film or share another team's screen or build without permission. Media consent is taken at check-in.
